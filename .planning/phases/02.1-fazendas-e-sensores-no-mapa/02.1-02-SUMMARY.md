# Phase 02.1 Plan 02: Farms and sensors schema (migration 006) Summary

Migration 006 adds farm boundaries (validated GeoJSON polygon, server-recomputed area and center), sensor coordinates and radius, a `register_sensor` RPC that seeds simulated hourly readings, and stops cloning the demo farm on signup. It is applied live and verified by an anon REST probe.

## Design notes

- Authenticated clients cannot update `boundary` after insert; the column grant covers only name/city/state.
- The boundary guard caps non-demo farms at 20 per owner.
- `register_sensor` curves keep new sensors within the default thresholds (temperature 10-32, air humidity 30-85, soil moisture 25-80).
